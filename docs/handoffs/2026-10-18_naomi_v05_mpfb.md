  All are inside the 33 ms (30 fps) budget. **Flag for the cloud thread:** v04 has the same triangle count as v03 (44.8k against 44.7k in the GLB), yet the scene renders about 12 times the primitives and 17 times the draw calls. Something the v04 runtime adds, probably `CharacterAnimationRig`/`CharacterFaceRig` or the alpha brow/lash surfaces in the shadow passes, multiplies the passes. Investigate it before adding more characters.
- **Face vertices line up:** 4,112 of v03's 5,194 body vertices sit exactly on the MPFB v02 base, because `build_naomi_v03.py` protects the head from decimation and only poses the arms. So MPFB targets can be carried across without rerunning the v03 build.
- **New step `tools/character_animation/bake_mpfb_face_shapes.py`:** runs after `build_animated_character.py`. It loads MPFB2 faceunit and viseme `.target` files on the v02 base body, then carries each offset to the built body. Exact matches copy the offset; added lash and brow verts take inverse-distance weights from the 4 nearest base verts, fading out after 12 mm. It writes new files only.
- **Mapping in `tools/character_animation/mpfb_face_mapping.json`:** the 10 stable names (blink, blink_L/R, mouth_open and the six cue expressions) are built from ARKit faceunits, and the 22 visemes are added as `viseme_<name>`.
- **Output:** `Naomi_Bennett_v05_mpfb.blend/.glb` (32 morphs), manifests, a face report, `CharacterVisualPreview` entry `v05_mpfb`, and review captures in `ingame_review_v05_mpfb/`.
- **Visible change against v04:** blink now closes fully, with no white gap. `mouth_open` reads clearly at the counter. Expressions remain subtle.
