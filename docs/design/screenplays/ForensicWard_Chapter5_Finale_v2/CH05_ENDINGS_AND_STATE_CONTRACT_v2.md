# Chapter 5 — Ending and state contract (PROPOSAL)

**No code or existing save schema is changed by this document.** Codex must inspect current F1/F2/F3 interfaces and choose identifiers compatible with the actual repository. Proposed state keys below are semantic concepts, not approved JSON names.

## Gate 1: Clinical responsibility
All safety-critical clinical tasks are completed or documented as appropriately escalated with receiving team and deadline. These tasks never require supernatural discovery. A clinically inappropriate choice triggers corrective opportunity and realistic consequences, not a secret paranormal branch.

## Gate 2: Evidence
Evidence records preserve provenance, acquisition method, clock source, source dependencies, chain of custody and distinction between observation and hypothesis. The system must not count two derived exports from the same camera or database as two independent sources.

## Gate 3: Institutional review
A factual report is generated with known findings, unresolved facts, safety measures and review obligations. Personal trust influences cooperation/optional lines but cannot block required reports or mandatory clues.

## Ending selection
- **A / Independent Review:** Owner selects referral to qualified outside reviewers, with evidence preserved and appropriate existing safeguards.
- **B / Restricted Access:** Owner selects indefinite restricted access with preserved evidence and a documented periodic review mechanism.
- **C / Continuing Inquiry:** Owner selects bounded additional investigation with independent oversight and explicit safety stop conditions.

All three endings are available after the essential gates; no personality score or supernatural belief requirement. Ending-specific epilogues reference evidence quality and earlier relationships as variants, not eligibility tests.

## Persisted narrative dimensions (proposed)
- Protagonist appearance, pronouns, prior professional background, and moderate personal-life details.
- Relationship interactions with Bennett, Voss, Hale, Rahman as nuanced dialogue histories, not morality alignment.
- Mara's independent treatment and agency state.
- Chapter-scoped verified evidence, unresolved hypotheses and institutional decisions.
- Ending decision and epilogue variant.

## Minimum test plan before implementation
1. Each ending is reachable from a valid save independent of personality selections.
2. Clinical safety gate cannot be bypassed and never waits on Ward Zero evidence.
3. Different backgrounds retain access to all mandatory clues.
4. Shared-source copies do not count as independent evidence.
5. A failed recording or zone load cannot soft-lock the finale; F2 rollback returns to a verified safe scene.
6. Saving/reloading at the ending decision preserves all selections and provenance.
7. Historical save migrations do not corrupt existing Chapter 1 state.
8. Clinical fields remain review-gated and never auto-approved by narrative dialogue.
9. Anomalies do not depend on patient diagnosis, symptom burden or treatment decisions.
10. All narrative clues have accessible text alternatives where audio/visual cues are used.
