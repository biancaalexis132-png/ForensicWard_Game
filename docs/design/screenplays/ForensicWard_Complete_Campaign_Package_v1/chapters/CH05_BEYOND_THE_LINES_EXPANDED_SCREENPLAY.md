# CHAPTER 5 — BEYOND THE LINES
## Expanded end-to-end playable screenplay | production draft v1.0

> **Status:** Proposed narrative/production manuscript. Not a shipped Godot runtime or a clinically approved treatment simulation. Source baseline: GitHub five-chapter draft and later Chapter 2–5 creative expansions.

## Chapter intent
The psychiatrist helps the institution choose one of three responsible responses; patient care and independent evidence remain separate.

## Full ending epilogues

### A — Independent Review
The institution transmits authenticated evidence to qualified external reviewers under a documented referral. The record lists unresolved questions, retained safety restrictions, an accountable owner for responses, and a scheduled status report. No one declares a known physical cause.
**VOSS:** We are sending the record, not a story about the record.
**PLAYER:** Then we give the reviewers what they need to challenge our findings.
**HALE:** And the restricted area stays restricted during that review.

### B — Restricted Access
The sublevel closes under maintained safety orders. All original evidence is retained. Oversight receives a scheduled review date, a named responsible office, reopening criteria and a process to reconsider the restriction. This is not a cover-up or a claim the anomaly was solved.
**HALE:** The door stays closed. The review does not stop.
**PLAYER:** Then document when the decision will be reconsidered.
**VOSS:** We will. Uncertainty does not release us from oversight.

### C — Continuing Inquiry
A bounded investigation is chartered with an explicit question set, external oversight, checkpoints, a budget owner and stop conditions. Patient-care coverage continues. A new inspection requires separate authorization; this ending does not imply unlimited physical access.
**RAHMAN:** Who decides whether further testing is justified?
**PLAYER:** The independent oversight group, under written limits.
**VOSS:** And the first unsafe condition ends the operation.

### Common epilogue
On the following morning, Naomi stands at the nursing station amid routine work. She looks up and waits until the psychiatrist is close.
**BENNETT:** Morning, Doctor. Ready for rounds?
**PLAYER:** Who are we seeing first?
**BENNETT:** Whoever needs us.
The player may glance at an optional personal message that reflects chosen background, but cannot change the ending retroactively. Fade out.
