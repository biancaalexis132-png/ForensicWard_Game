# CHAPTER 2 — THE MISSING HOURS
## Expanded end-to-end playable screenplay | production draft v1.0

> **Status:** Proposed narrative/production manuscript. Not a shipped Godot runtime or a clinically approved treatment simulation. Source baseline: GitHub five-chapter draft and later Chapter 2–5 creative expansions.

## Chapter intent
Routine care and institutional records investigation converge, without enlisting Mara as a mystery-solving device.

## Scene 01 — Back in the Routine
**Location:** Clinical unit
**Function:** The next shift begins with regular work. Ward Zero is now an information-security matter; Mara Ellis enters as a patient with her own reasons to seek care.

### Screenplay & blocking
**BENNETT**: The audit office has the ticket. So do we, until somebody owns it.  
**PLAYER**: Any patient changes overnight?  
**BENNETT**: Yes. Those come first.

- **B.** Review handoff
- **C.** Check audit status
