# Character, choice and ending bible — proposed production specification

## Psychiatrist protagonist
Fixed psychiatrist role, with appearance, voice if feasible, name and pronouns selectable. Backgrounds are professional experiences, not classes. Personal-life customization is moderate and entirely optional. Personality is inferred from behavior and contextualized over time, not a diagnostic trait or a morality meter. Prevent loss of core clinical competence, mandatory story evidence or major ending opportunities based on selections.

## Ensemble
**Naomi Bennett:** Charge Nurse; pragmatic, observant, warm and dry-humored. Her trust grows through reliable clinical teamwork, especially accurate sign-out, not obedience. **Dr. Evelyn Voss:** senior hospital leader; clear institutional responsibilities and honest procedural tension, not automatic villainy. **Dr. Leila Rahman:** Consultant Psychiatrist, separate clinical expertise and judgment; not a pharmacist. **Marcus Hale:** Security Director, professional security and access authority, not militarized. **Elias Ward:** 29-year-old competency-restoration transfer; minimally responsive, needs evidence-informed medical and psychiatric assessment; not a supernatural emissary. **Mara Ellis:** an established deeper dossier elsewhere in the project discussion; do not overwrite or assign her to the provisional second patient slot without approved chapter placement. **Jordan Vale:** Chapter 1 illustrative second patient only, unapproved.

## Arc over five chapters
- Ch1: trust through care, one implausible transfer request, administrative hypotheses remain viable.
- Ch2: independent timing discrepancies and the value of source reliability.
- Ch3: historic Ward Zero, contradictions in records and space, increasing independent verification.
- Ch4: controlled, reproducible contradictions; institutional responsibilities and ethical disclosure.
- Ch5: evidence-respecting investigation and responsible institutional response; mystery’s precise mechanism remains unknown.

## Three proposed ending variants
**Independent Review:** player secures external independent verification; carefully reported anomaly; safeguarding maintained. **Restricted Access:** leadership restricts Ward Zero pending formal investigation with audit retained, not destroyed. **Continuing Inquiry:** incomplete evidence requires transparent continued review; no false paranormal proclamation. All endings maintain patient care and factual documentation; which ending occurs depends on documented institutional decisions and relationship credibility rather than arbitrary personality points. The genuine supernatural phenomenon is not denied in the story world, but exact mechanism need not be explained.

## Proposed narrative persistence (do not implement without owner approval)
Chapter-specific audit records, evidence source confidence, colleagues' professional respect/trust, voluntarily provided personal-contact preferences and selected background. Keep clinical record state independent from environmental anomaly state. Never attach paranormal causality to mental status diagnoses, medication responses or trauma.
