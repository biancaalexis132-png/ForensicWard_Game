# Chapter 4 — The Official Record: Evidence and Major Reveal
**Expanded draft v2 | Chapter 4 major corridor-loop placement intentionally preserved; exact mechanics pending owner approval.**

Chapter 4 owns the first **major reproducible spatial loop** in the original merged screenplay (original Scene 09, “The Corridor That Repeats”). Chapter 3's route concerns and surveys are setup, not the reveal. The supernatural phenomenon is real in the game's world, but staff respond through evidence, safety restrictions, institutional procedure and patient-centered care.

## Scene 01 — The morning after
Voss convenes Hale, Facilities and the protagonist. Historical transfer audit and spatial survey are separate incident files.

**VOSS:** We are not here to settle a theory. We have observations we can't yet reconcile and a restricted area with possible safety implications.
**ENGINEER:** Some old plans are incomplete. Other differences remain unexplained.
**HALE:** Nobody enters pending the independent safety review.
**PLAYER:** The inactive-credential transfer request remains an information-security question.
**VOSS:** Precisely. Don't join two incomplete cases just because their labels resemble each other.

Choices: preserve transparent findings; focus on access restrictions; request outside expertise. None bypasses safety responsibilities.

## Scene 02 — The report nobody wants to sign
A junior summary describes the area as a “confirmed mapping error,” though no review has proven that cause.

**PLAYER:** The administrative summary says it was a mapping error. Facilities says unresolved.
**VOSS:** Then the summary is wrong. Correct it. Keep the first version in the revision history.

Interactive editorial choices: state the observed contradiction; prematurely attribute it to mapping error; claim supernatural geometry. Revision allowed. Only well-supported language can be entered as the final official finding.

## Scene 03 — Rahman's clinical boundary
**RAHMAN:** An unusual facilities report doesn't change the standards for evaluating a patient who is distressed or sleeping poorly.
**PLAYER:** And an old psychiatric diagnosis doesn't prove an audit record false.
**RAHMAN:** Both are important. Different questions need different evidence.

Clinical activity remains separately authored and approved. No anomaly state changes psychiatric diagnosis.

## Scene 04 — Mara's own follow-up
Mara asks whether her aunt's record dates have been explained. The authorized review remains open. Her care plan concerns reported fatigue, anxiety, function and appropriate follow-up, regardless of the records' eventual explanation.

**MARA:** Did they decide one of the dates was wrong?
**PLAYER:** The records differ. The reason hasn't been established.
**MARA:** I wish somebody had said that the first time.
**PLAYER:** I'd also like to talk about how the insomnia has been affecting you.

No revelation through patient symptoms; no patient confidential material exported into incident evidence.

## Scene 05 — The paper trail fails
An outside reviewer compares retained originals and identifies a time-order contradiction after known export display differences are taken into account; independence is still documented rather than assumed.

**ANALYST:** I can't make these versions agree without excluding one of the originals.
**PLAYER:** Then preserve them both. Identify which checks were performed.

This is independent-record scrutiny, not yet the spatial-loop climax.

## Scene 09 — The Corridor That Repeats: MAJOR REVEAL (proposed execution)
**CRITICAL PLACEMENT:** This is the original manuscript's Chapter 4 major loop event. Do not shift it to Chapter 3. Only stage after safety, authorization and **F2 safe-return contract** have passed implementation review.

A controlled inspection has an explicit stop-and-return protocol, verified escort roster, operational-side contact and rescue fallback. Separate survey teams record landmarks using independently acquired observations rather than a single engine-provided truth source copied to several displays.

At the cleared threshold, the team makes one linear outward transit. It observes a distinct numbered wall defect. Continuing along the permitted corridor without turning, the team encounters a matching defect in what appears to be the same junction. This time independent route and access evidence, gathered through separately controlled paths, excludes the simple mistaken-sign explanation used in Chapter 3. The player is **not** permitted to loop endlessly.

**ESCORT:** That damaged panel. We already passed it.
**PLAYER:** Stop. Confirm our position and use the documented return procedure.
**HALE (RADIO):** Suspend the test. Preserve both raw observation streams. Begin safe return.

Options (all safe): compare separately acquired records; declare stop condition; request independent analyst review. They affect dialogue and later reporting, not access to an exit.

**Hard guardrails:** bounded one-time dramatic replay, escape immediately available, no player softlock, no dynamic corridor reshuffle after rollback begins, no patient-mental-state trigger, no claim that two logs from the same anomaly system constitute independent corroboration. If instrument independence is not implemented, narrative labels must remain `observed_repetition_unverified`, not `independently_corroborrated`.

## Scene 10 — The official record
Team safely exits; original evidence is preserved; controlled technical review may conclude there is a reproducible spatial inconsistency **only when the independent-evidence gate actually passes**. The staff do not assert a supernatural mechanism in hospital clinical charts.

**VOSS:** Whatever the final explanation, we won't make the discrepancy disappear by changing the wording.
**HALE:** Area remains restricted. The original observations stay intact.
**PLAYER:** And patients continue to receive their care without their histories becoming part of the building investigation.

The final institutional choice is how to maintain oversight, independent inquiry and safety. Chapter 5 retains the final responsible-response pathways in the previously merged ending bible.
