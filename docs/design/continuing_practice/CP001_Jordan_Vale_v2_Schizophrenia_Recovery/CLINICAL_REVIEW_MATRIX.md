# CP-001 — Clinical content signoff matrix

No item is approved for clinical scoring simply because the owner's high-level diagnosis direction is approved.

| Content domain | Proposed | Pending specialist reviewer check | Allowed before review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Historical schizophrenia diagnosis | established fictional chart history | longitudinal source, diagnostic accuracy, wording | narrative summary in proposal docs only |
| Mood and anxiety symptoms | Jordan describes worry and sometimes feeling low | duration, severity, differential from negative symptoms, sleep issues, psychosis | unscored proposed dialogue only |
| Sleep/fatigue/function | ~2 weeks interrupted sleep; difficulty attending art | medical differential and risk screening | source-attributed fiction in docs |
| Medication | patient requests explanation and worries about grogginess | reconcile medication/MAR, contraindications and response | no drugs, doses or pharmacologic effects |
| Positive/negative psychosis | not pre-authored current assessment | current presentation, history, MSE, meaningful longitudinal course | never assume symptoms present/absent |
| Risk | not pre-authored risk findings | appropriate, encounter-specific risk prompts and escalation | no risk score or mandatory negative response |
| Legal/forensic | secure unit only | authority, current legal status, permissions | do not assign crimes, incompetence or dangerousness |
| Collateral | sister Sam proposed | consent, legal basis, privacy, exact approved dialogue | no unauthorized disclosures |
| Capacity | no incapacity finding | decision-specific assessment only if relevant | no capacity ruling |
| Recovery | patient-defined sleep, activity, participation, understanding | measurement and potential confounders | functional goals, no guarantee of improvement |
| Notes/handoff | append-only notes and explicit acknowledged follow-up | documentation rubric and recipient roles | design contract only |

**Acceptance invariants:** no diagnostic points for guessing schizophrenia; no false attribution of collateral; no psychosis/supernatural coupling; respectful refusal allowed; medications/labs/vitals absent until authored; no forced discharge; three endings and any protagonist background can access CP-001; completing five encounters does not automatically assert remission.
