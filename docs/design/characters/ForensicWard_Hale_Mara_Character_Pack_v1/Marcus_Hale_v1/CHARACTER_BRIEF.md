# Marcus Hale — Character model and performance brief

**Identity:** `staff_hale`, **Security Director** at Ashcroft State Forensic Hospital. Recurring staff character; not a tactical soldier, occultist or automatic antagonist. Runtime staff entry exists in `data/staff.json`; voice profile exists in `audio/voice_profiles.json`. Production visual details here are proposals until creator approves.

**Appearance proposal:** man in his mid-40s; practical, composed, physically credible and professional; short neat hair, optional closely trimmed facial hair, restrained facial animation. Final ethnicity, facial likeness and build require owner approval. Neutral uniform-style shirt or professional blazer with facility ID badge and an ordinary radio on belt. Muted navy and charcoal palette; comfortable institutional footwear. No plate carrier, weapons display, riot equipment, overt villain styling, occult emblems, or assumptions about personality based on physical appearance.

**Acting language:** listens before answering; careful stance; modest shift of weight; subtle glance to entry reader/radio during access discussions; calm response to routine requests. Do not assign a 'suspicious' expression by default or turn security policy into supernatural explanation. He can disagree with the protagonist without losing professional dignity.

**Rigging and staging:** standing neutral rig + conversational animation set; later seated version if scene calls for it. Idle, turn-to-speaker, gaze change, acknowledgment, speaking, pause, calm refusal, radio check, walk and stop. Radio check should be optional and never emit an access grant. Avoid animated clipping with badge/lanyard. No animation may alter access permissions on its own.

**Godot contract:** preserve `staff_hale` ID, dialogue and access-grant logic. Spawn and scenes follow authored content, not this doc. Mesh replacement only after validated Godot import and no regression to stand-ins/interactions. Separate character script/output folder `tools/blender/characters/hale/` proposed. Avoid editing Naomi's working branch. LOD/texture budgets are proposals until profiling.
