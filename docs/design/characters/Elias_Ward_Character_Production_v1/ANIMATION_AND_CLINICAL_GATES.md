# Elias Ward — animation and clinical review gates

## Cleared for visual prototyping (not clinical claims)
- Quiet neutral seated posture, natural breathing, blink, gaze shifts, routine neutral idle, clothing movement if cost permits.
- Non-diagnostic neutral facial expressions with no forced threat/horror cues.
- Conservative staff/patient personal-space behavior consistent with authored interview scene.

## NOT cleared without owner psychiatrist review
- Any representation of catatonia, posturing, waxy flexibility, echophenomena, negativism, motor freezing, altered level of consciousness, or effects of medication/intervention.
- Any specific symptom severity score or scripted treatment response.
- Any cue that equates lack of speech with inability to understand, lacks decision-making capacity, or implies violence.
- Any supernatural trigger caused by patient symptoms, psychotropic response or rapport.
