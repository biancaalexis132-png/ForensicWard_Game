## Shared visual protections

Ashcroft is a functioning, maintained psychiatric hospital. Present people as ordinary professionals and patients, without ominous portrait lighting, restraints as character identity, symbolic props or “Ward Zero patient” supernatural labeling. Correct ages, ordinary grooming, appropriate clothing and humane treatment outrank cinematic horror aesthetics. No unauthorized actor or identifiable real-person likenesses.

Exact faces, skin-tone values, heights, body proportions, religion/nationality, accessory variants and hairstyles beyond the written constraints are not canonized by this table. New generated alternatives are candidates for owner review. In particular, the guide does not specify ethnicity for Voss, Hale, Elias or Mara; do not infer final casting from a generated face. Suggested heights, hex palettes and added props in the original review remain proposals. Do not treat a generated multi-view sheet as geometrically verified orthographic artwork.
