Beyond the Lines

Panel p01

The final operation begins with witnesses, explicit limits, and adequate coverage for the patients upstairs.

Panel p02

The accumulated record is examined as a whole. Even now, some questions remain unresolved.

Panel p03

What matters is not certainty at any cost. It is whether the next action can be defended, reviewed, and reversed when necessary.

Panel p04

Different responsibilities meet at one table. Safety, access, oversight, and care all have to be considered.

Panel p05

The institution must decide how to respond to findings it cannot yet explain. The choice belongs to accountable people, not a single rumor.
